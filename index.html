Questions Before You Delegate

Will I lose control of my calendar?

Set booking windows, meeting limits, and approval rules first. Conflicting or sensitive requests should come back to you.

Can an assistant handle confidential messages?

Define which messages they may access and use individual accounts. Keep restricted conversations outside their assigned permissions.

Does remote mailing support include packing envelopes?

Remote support covers records and coordination. Physical printing, packing, and postage require your onsite team or a mailing provider.

What if explaining tasks takes too much time?

Begin with one repeatable workflow and a completed example. Review the first results before adding more responsibilities.
