Set Expectations Before Delegating

Can coordination follow our Central Time hours?

Specify the coverage window and required response times. Confirm availability before assigning tasks that need live communication.

What if an email needs a business decision?

Define which replies are routine and which require approval. Pricing commitments, complaints, and sensitive matters should follow your escalation rules.

Does remote support include physical mailing?

Remote work covers preparation and coordination. Your onsite staff or mailing provider handles printing, packaging, and postage.

Can we begin without transferring every responsibility?

Start with a small group of repeatable tasks. Review accuracy and turnaround before adding responsibilities or wider access.
